# Museum bake pipeline

Mechanical tools: they never change the approved room layout. `pipeline.sh` runs everything (see its header).

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| textures | `grade_textures.py`, `floor_synth.py`, `plank_variation.py`, `floor_tone.py`, `props_atlas.py` | grade the Poly Haven textures (cream-beige paint), rebuild the floor as random-length boards (2k, 2.2 m tile), tone it dark walnut-cherry; paint the one texture for the rugs, marble, gift wrap, boxwood and the balloon colours (`decor/decor_atlas.jpg`). Already done: the results are in `assets-src/blender/textures/` |
| look | `design.py` | palette, tray ceilings with cornice + gold lines, a few downlights, track spots sized to every photo and exhibit, collage niche with LEDs, gold wall panels, soft wall wash, a dimmer screen room (like a little cinema), and the warm glow of every candle, lantern and the neon (accent lights). Light colours and levels at the top (`WARM_WHITE`, `LEVEL`) |
| furniture and decor | `props.py` (used by `design.py`) with `props_romance.py`, `props_flowers.py`, `props_trees.py`, `props_cinema.py`, `props_geometry.py` | everything in the rooms and the garden, modelled in code and merged into one mesh per material in each part of the museum. Where everything stands: `LAYOUT` in `props.py` (each exhibit also names the rail its own spot hangs from); colours: `PALETTE`. Every piece gets a `footprint_<name>` marker for walking and the petals (a circle for round pieces, else a box, covering what stands on the floor up to head height); the `candle_flames` marker lists where the web lights a flame, `projector_lens` where the projector's beam starts |
| garden | `garden.py` (used by `design.py`) with `garden_layout.py`, models from `garden_assets.py` (+ `garden_wood.py`, `garden_cushions.py`: the teak bench and its cushions, the firewood and the burnt earth, made in code) | the night garden: CC0 models from Poly Haven (ASSETS.md), placed in code (where everything stands: `garden_layout.py`), merged per material into `prop_garden_*`; footprints, the seats, candle flames, the `campfire` marker and the fire's baked light, the tree's little lights (`garden_lights`) and their baked glow; the grass and the garden walls' tiling textures. `garden_assets.py` prepares the downloads once (size, triangles, 256–512 px textures) into `assets-src/blender/textures/garden/`: run it only to change a model (raw downloads are kept outside the repo) |
| entrance | `entrance.py` (used by `design.py`) with pieces from `props_entrance.py` (placed by `LAYOUT` in `props.py`) and its wood from `garden.py` (`Garden.entrance`, `ENTRANCE` in `garden_layout.py`) | the clearing outside the foyer's south door where the couple start (`spawn_her`, `spawn_him` moved there): a stone path across a lawn that runs on under a little wood (`prop_entrance_*`, vertex light), little lights strung over it (in `garden_lights`), the invisible ground the web walks on (`col_floor_entrance*`) and invisible walls round it; the ivory doorway with its red doors opened right back to the wall in a garland of roses, a wide red carpet into the foyer lined with gold flower stands, roses and balloons. The clearing's size, its wood (rows of trees and bushes, kept clear of the museum's walls) and its little lights: `ENTRANCE` in `garden_layout.py` |
| bake | `bake_lightmaps.py` | Cycles bake of walls, floors, ceilings, garden walls (light only, no colour) |
| encode | `finish_lightmaps.py` | denoise, PNG lightmaps + `lightmaps.json`, `light_rgb` on every photo slot |
| trim | `bake_trim_vertex.py`, `smooth_trim_light.py` | light for mouldings, cornices, the niche and all the furniture and decor, baked into vertex colours |
| check | `check_lightmaps.py` | quick render of the baked result |
| export | `export_glb.py` | museum.glb (custom properties on, lights off, vertex colours on) |

If you change the layout in Blender, edit `assets-src/blender/museum_base.blend` and run the pipeline again (~1 h on 2 CPU cores). To move or add furniture and decor, edit `LAYOUT` in `props.py` (the garden: `garden.py`) and run the pipeline.
