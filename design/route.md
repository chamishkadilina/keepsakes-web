## The walk

1. **Foyer:** enter from the south. Welcome wall ahead. Door to the story gallery in the north-east corner.
2. **Story gallery:** walk around the freestanding partition (x = 5.5, y 13.15–17.35). Photos on both sides. Leave east into the heart room.
3. **Heart room:** the big tall room. Heart photo wall on the north wall, glowing as you enter. Leave east.
4. **Right gallery:** the north wing runs east to the end window and back (a small loop), then go down the narrow west strip.
5. **Screen room:** dark and cosy. Slideshow screen and bench. The arch on the east wall glows.
6. **Garden:** twilight, roses, stone path, the centrepiece photo on the easel, a bench for two. The end.

## Wayfinding rule

The next door is always the brightest thing in the room. Petal trail (`path_*`) on the floor along this route.
